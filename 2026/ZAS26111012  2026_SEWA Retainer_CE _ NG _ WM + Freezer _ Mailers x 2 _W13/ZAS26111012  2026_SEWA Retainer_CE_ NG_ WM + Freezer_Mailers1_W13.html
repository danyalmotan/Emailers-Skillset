The Samsung Chest Freezer offers
minimalist styling in an elegant dark grey
colour that gives it a modern and luxurious
look and feel. Unlike traditional white and
silver finishes, it stays trendy and blends
unobtrusively into its surroundings.
  
Available at all authorized Samsung stores nationwide.

     

      
EasyFreeze Control
         
Control the freezer temperature quickly
and easily. It can be adjusted in 8 steps
from 1 to Max* by turning the EasyFreeze
Control, with each step reducing the
temperature in small increments down to
the lowest level of -25°C.

    

 
    
Fits in efficiently
      
Avoid wasting space with only a small gap
behind the chest freezer. The door has a
thin rear hinge, so the freezer can be
installed close to the wall.
