Max Heat
 
Stay comfortably warm all year round even in extremely cold
conditions. Max Heat ensures that the air conditioner provides a
100% heating performance when it is as low as -20°C outside.
It will even continue working reliably and heat your home
effectively in freezing temperatures of -30°C^^
 
* Based on internal testing on the AR**CSDACWKXCV model.

Test conditions: indoor (OB 21.1°C / WB 15.6°C], Outdoor [DB -20°C).
